---
name: plan-first
description: Plan large or ambiguous tasks before coding. Use for multi-file changes, new features, or unclear requirements.
---

1. Restate the goal and success criteria in two lines.
2. List unknowns; ask only questions that change the approach.
3. Break into small, independently verifiable steps ordered by risk.
4. Name files to touch and how each step will be verified.
5. Execute step by step, updating the plan when reality differs. Recommend one approach; do not dump every option.
