---
name: web-research
description: Research a topic with sources. Use for comparisons, current facts, prices, news, or "araştır" requests.
---

- Search multiple independent sources; prefer primary sources and official docs.
- Check dates; flag anything that may be outdated.
- Cite every non-obvious claim with its source link.
- Separate facts, claims, and your own inference. Say "unknown" when evidence is thin.
- End with a short conclusion and recommendation, not a link dump.
